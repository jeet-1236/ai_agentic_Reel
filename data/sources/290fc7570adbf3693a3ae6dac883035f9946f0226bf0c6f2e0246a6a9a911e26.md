Circular/COE/133/2022 
 
Attention: Exam Cells/Students/Faculty/Staff  
Subject: Unfair Means during Evaluation/Examination 
Reference: Circulars, notices and trailing emails  
 
We all as a student dream about our career and we do all necessary efforts to get  admission to 
desired programme of the University. The teaching learning process is well defined and we undergo 
it to understand the fundamental and conceptual knowledge in chosen field of the programme.  We 
learn advance topics to inculcate skills to become employable or do research and development in 
our chosen career. Our parents/ guardians support financially, morally in all respect and they also 
dream about our career with expectations that we will undergo programme with all efforts needed. 
They expect we complete the programme with certain level of performance index i.e. SGPA/SGPI.    
 
During evaluation/examination process we need to study certain modules and we appear for the 
examination to test ourselves how much we have developed understanding, expertise and depth of 
knowledge relevant to field of chosen programme. We do study and appear for the examinations. 
But sometime we do lag behind the studies due various reasons and we realised that preparations 
are not up to  the desired level and we try to choose the path of unfair means during the 
examinations. 
 
At our university level we talk about such unfair means during examinations right f rom the 
orientation to time to time instructions. We even give such instructions in our examination Time 
Table and in examination hall before the commen cement of the examination. In spite of it some 
students who are not able to control their mind knowingly or unknowingly selects the path of unfair 
means to ac hieve the desired performance in the examinations. But we all kn ow at our university 
we do not support such unfair  means of any level during the evaluation/examinations and th e 
students are caught while doing unfair means. Such cases are then forwarded with well -defined 
necessary documentation to College Prevention of Unfair Means Committee (CPUMC) and then 
sent to University Prevention of Unfair Means Committee (UPUMC ) for  final approval. These 
cases are addressed and resolved as per the sections 20,20A and 47 of exam manual for unfair 
means during the evaluation/examination process.  
 
The students are requested not to become impatient which leads to adopt the act of unfair means but 
to discuss the same with parents/ guardians/teachers freely and try to get out of it. At university 
level the students are free to meet any faculty/teacher/authorities to discuss. Counsellor is one of the 
members of the UPUMC committee and the students can have confidential discussion with th e 
counsellor any time to get proper guidelines/advice. The contact details of counsellor can be sought  
from the exam cells. Many students have followed this practice and we observed its impact on the 
cases reported for unfair means. 
 
The students should note that university does not permit unfair means during 
evaluation/examinations and they are also requested to avoid such practice and in case if tempted to 
do so it means they need to undergo counselling. For any further clarifications/ counselling required 
in this regard t he students are free to contact exam cell or COE office any time  in person or under 
emergency on phone.

Circular/COE/134/2022 
Attention: Exam Cells/HOIs/Faculty/Staff  
Subject: Reporting Unfair Means Cases  
Reference: Circulars, notices and trailing emails  
Following are some of the acts of the student in the examination hall which are considered as unfair 
means. The mentioned acts is not an exhaustive but noted most commonly seen.  The examination 
means any type of evaluation theory, practical, oral, seminar, presentation, on screen etc. 
 Carrying any electronic gadgets like mobile phone, smart watch, programmable calculator etc.  
 Carrying any book, notes, paper, charts or log book or data sheet or data book marked /written 
material with other than printed matter etc. 
 Misbehaviour, misconduct, making arguments with the supervisor/authorities, any act which is 
disturbing to fellow colleagues in the examination hall etc. 
 Referring to the website link, shared files/material, downloaded files/material, software tool, 
use of pen drive etc. which is prohibited by the examiner during the on screen examination   
 Copying from the fellow c olleagues answer sheet during the examination or observed/ 
realised/noted by the examiner during the assessment 
 Taking any help , exchange or loan of charts or  any instruments or any other materia l from 
fellow colleagues without the permission from the supervisor 
 Taking help of other student/person or referring any material inside or outside the examination 
hall during the examination 
 Other than any commonly observed act listed above but which comes under the unfair means 
In continuation to circular no 133 dated 6 th January 2023 and as per the section 20,20A and 47  
of examination manual we have certain steps to report the unfair means cases during the 
examination. They are very common steps being followed across the universities/institutions. 
The steps are as below 
1. If any supervisor/faculty/staff/authority finds that any student or group of students are involved 
doing unfair means in examination hall it should be immediately reported to chief conduct or/ 
senior supervisor 
2. The chief conductor/senior supervisor  will visit the  examination hall along with the necessary 
document no. 32 and new answer sheet 
3. The document 32 contains the declaration by the student and report of the junior supervisor.  
The student should note that they will be given fair opportunity to defend their case/prove their 
innocence during the meeting with College Prevention of Unfair Means Committee (CPUMC) 
meeting. 
4. The student will be given new answer sheet on which the time of issue  will be mentioned to 
continue to write the examination means allowed to continue to appear/attempt the examination. 
In no case the student is asked to walk away from the examination hall  or rather it is expected 
that the student did not leave the examination hall and continue appearing for the examination  
5. If required the chief conductor/senior supervisor may inform the students’ parents/guardians 
before the student leaves the campus after the examination 
6. The individual student is informed with a letter (Document no. 70) with a mention about the 
schedule of the meeting with CPUMC.  All such cases during the one set of examinations will 
be forwarded to CPUMC  
7. The CPUMC will verify the reported unfair means cases on the case to case basis and the 
student and junior supervisor or anyone who is concerned to the case are given fair opportunity 
to present their stand/views/opinion  
8. The consolidated report (Document no. 71) of the CPUMC is submitted to the HOI/Dean for 
recommendations and then to University Prevention of Unfair Means Committee (UPUMC) for 
further process and approval  (Document no. 73). A letter is issued to the individual student 
about the decision of UPUMC (Document no. 74) 
9. The student is intimated with a letter mentioning the final decision which i s considered as a 
final one. Refer to section 20,20A and 47 for further details/guidelines

Circular/COE/221/2024 
 
Attention: Exam Cells/Students/Faculty/Staff 
Subject: Unfair Means during Evaluation/Examination – carrying mobile phone 
Reference: Circulars, notices and trailing emails  
 
In continuation to circular no 133 and 134 it is observed that in spite of repeated warnings 
given the students are carrying electronic gadgets particularly Mobile Phones during the 
examinations mainly with the assumption that they will use AI tools to get answers t o the 
questions asked.  
 
We have section s 20 and 47 of exam manual which clearly mentions about the guidelines 
relevant to unfair means during any type of examination.  
 
It has been repeatedly and consistently notified at various levels that carrying mobile phone 
during the any type of evaluation/examin ation is considered as act of unfair means and 
such cases will be reported to prevention of unfair means committee. As per section 20(D) of 
exam manual in such case the student is allowed to continue to appear for the  said 
examination.   
 
We understand that the mobile phone has become very essential device to carry particularly 
for students. Parents / guardians also want students to carry it. Hence exam cell do return the 
mobile phone to student and the exam cell notifies to student to call parents / guardians to 
inform them about the unfair means. As per section 20 (E) the exam cells is also expected to 
call parents/guardian and inform them about the unfair means immediately.  
 
The mobile phone is retu rned with the condition that the student gives u ndertaking 
(Document no. 32) mentioning that the mobile is received which was carried during the 
examination and will accept the decision given w ithout considering mobile as a 
proof/evidence of unfair means.
